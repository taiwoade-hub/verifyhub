Traditional tools often produce disconnected results. Kane connects the dots between domains, infrastructure, and identities so you can reason about what matters.

Connect domains, IPs, certificates, technologies and other entities.

Responsible intelligence.

Kane is designed around ethical principles. We believe intelligence work should be transparent, accountable, and respectful of boundaries.

Public sources

Only gathers from legitimate public and authorized data sources.

Authorized targets

Built for targets you own or have explicit permission to investigate.

Evidence-backed findings

Every finding includes source attribution and confidence scoring.

Rate limiting

Explicit permissions
